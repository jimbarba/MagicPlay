MagicPlay Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Overview

MagicPlay is designed to work without requiring a user account. The app does not collect personal information for advertising, analytics, or user profiling.

Data collection

MagicPlay does not collect or sell personal data.

Audio files, setlists, track settings, MIDI configurations and lighting configurations are used by the app to provide its functionality. These items are stored and processed on the user's device unless the user explicitly chooses to export or share configuration data.

Network connections

When Art-Net / DMX lighting control is used, MagicPlay sends network packets to the lighting equipment configured by the user. These connections are used solely to control the equipment and are not used to collect personal information.

Third-party services

MagicPlay does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

Children's privacy

MagicPlay is not directed at collecting information from children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the privacy practices of MagicPlay change, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Contact

For privacy questions regarding MagicPlay, please use the support contact provided with the application.
