MagicPlay Support

Getting started

Create or import your setlist, add your WAV tracks, adjust individual track levels, and use Concert Mode during your performance.

MIDI

MagicPlay can send MIDI Program Change and Control Change messages to compatible MIDI devices. MIDI actions can be associated with tracks and triggered as part of your setlist.

Lighting

MagicPlay can control compatible lighting equipment through Art-Net / DMX. Fixtures, groups and scenes can be configured and associated with tracks.

Import and export

Use the configuration import and export features to move your MagicPlay setup between supported devices or keep a backup of your configuration.

Need help?

If you encounter a problem, please provide the MagicPlay version, your iPhone or iPad model, iOS/iPadOS version, and a description of the issue.
