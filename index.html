MagicPlay

Your setlist. Your show.

MagicPlay is a live performance app for musicians. Organize your setlist, play WAV backing tracks, and control MIDI and Art-Net/DMX lighting from your iPhone or iPad.

Built for live performance

  • Setlists and WAV playback
  • Individual track volume
  • MIDI Program Change and Control Change
  • Art-Net / DMX lighting control
  • Scenes, fixtures and groups
  • MIDI and lighting actions associated with tracks
  • Concert Mode
  • Import and export of configurations
